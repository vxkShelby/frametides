Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Frametides Clipper is a private tool used by one person to upload clips to their own TikTok account (@frametides.clips).

What data is used

The app uses an access token for the owner's own TikTok account, granted by the owner through TikTok's authorization flow, solely to upload video drafts to that account.

What is collected

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. It has no visitors, no accounts for other users, no analytics and no advertising.

Storage

The access token and the video files are stored on the owner's own computer. Video files are deleted after publication. The token can be revoked at any time in TikTok's account settings.

Third parties

Data is sent only to TikTok, in order to upload the owner's video. No other third party receives it.

Contact

Send a message to the TikTok account @frametides.clips.
